= Practical Object-Oriented Ruby

== ch02

A larger chainring with a smaller cog produces a lower ratio. Lower
speeds and easier to pedal.

A smaller chainring with a larger cog produces a higher ratio. Higher
speeds and harder to pedal.

The same chainring and cog size on a smaller or larger wheel also
influences speed and how easy or hard it is to pedal.

See illustrations on the __assets directory.


    gear ratio = chainring / cog
wheel diameter = rim diameter + twice tire diameter
    gear inches = wheel diameter * gear ratio


rim and tire are rim and tire diameter. The author mentions in the text,
but the params rim and tire bear no indication that they are diameters.
