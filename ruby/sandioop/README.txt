= Practical Object-Oriented Ruby

== ch02

A larger chairning with a smaller cog produces a lower ratio. Lower
speeds and easier to pedal.

A smaller chainring with a larger cog produces a higher ratio.
Higher speeds and harder to pedal.

The same chainring and cog size on a smaller or larger wheel also
influences speed and how easy or hard it is to pedal.

See illustrations on the __assets directory.
